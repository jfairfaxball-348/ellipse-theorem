**Why this particular result may interest a research audience.** In geometric graph theory and computational geometry, a planar triangulation of a strictly convex point set may choose its diagonals. The geometry of the ellipse and an eligible shortest Euclidean skip-chord selection guarantee recursive metric ears. This is the specific research-level claim presented for Palomar's editorial floor. The paper derives an `O(n log n)` constructive algorithm in the exact-real comparison model; this complexity statement is **not separately Lean-formalised or Comparator-selected**.
